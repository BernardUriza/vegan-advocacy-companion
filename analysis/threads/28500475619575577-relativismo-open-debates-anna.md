A parent is responsible for a child without owning the child. A guardian answers for a dependent person and still cannot sell them. Responsibility lives in the duty; the title is the extra claim over the body. What animal property law mainly enforces is that one human holds the claim and another human may not take the animal. The same title is what lets the holder confine her, sell her and kill her.So the dog you don't want taken is protected by a duty owed to the dog, and that duty needs no title. The pig has the title on him and none of the duties. If what you want is someone accountable for a dependent animal, that points to a duty toward that animal. What makes it legitimate for a conscious being to exist as property?
