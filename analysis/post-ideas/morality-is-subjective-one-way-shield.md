# "Morality is subjective" — el relativismo es un escudo de un solo sentido

Status: Drafted (2026-09-19)
Capturado: 2026-09-19 (semilla propuesta por Claude sobre el moat; táctica #1 sin post raíz)

## Borrador del post (voz de Bernard)

> Sooner or later the debate here lands on the same line: "morality is subjective, so I don't have to justify anything to you."
>
> Notice who gets to use that line. Nobody says it when their car is taken, their wages are withheld, or their kid is pushed around. In those cases the person suddenly knows exactly what is owed to whom and expects everyone else to know it too. Subjectivity only shows up when the one on the losing end has fur or feathers.
>
> Take the claim seriously for a second anyway. If morality is just preference, then "it's fine to own and kill them" is also just a preference. It carries no more weight than mine. What decides the matter at that point is power over the animal's body, and the relativism turns out to be a description of that power dressed up as a philosophy.
>
> "I don't owe anyone a justification" is a way of leaving the title undefended and calling it a win. The question stays open either way: what makes it legitimate for a sentient being, someone with a point of view, to exist as property? An answer that works would survive being asked. A shrug is what people reach for when they suspect it won't.

## El argumento afilado (la jugada)

1. **Asimetría de uso.** El relativista jamás es relativista con su propia
   propiedad, su salario o sus hijos — ahí sabe exactamente qué se le debe. La
   "subjetividad" solo aparece para el lado que pierde. Eso delata que no es una
   tesis metaética: es un escudo.
2. **Tomarlo en serio lo hunde.** Si la moral es preferencia, "está bien poseerlos"
   es preferencia, sin más autoridad que la contraria. Lo único que decide entonces
   es el poder sobre el cuerpo del animal — y el relativismo queda como la
   descripción de ese poder disfrazada de filosofía. (No hay que refutar el
   relativismo: basta con mostrar que no le sirve al que lo invoca.)
3. **"No debo justificar" ≠ justificar.** Dejar el título sin defender no lo
   defiende ([[retreat-cycle-defense]] estación 2). La pregunta queda de pie.
4. **Vuelta al hueso** — propiedad de un sujeto que siente (`algo-a-alguien-sujeto-derecho`,
   eje [[abolitionist-framing]] + [[sentiocentrism-not-biocentrism]]).
