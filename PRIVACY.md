# Privacy notice (aviso de privacidad)

This repository keeps a **longitudinal memory of people who take part in public
Facebook debates** about veganism (`data/actors.json`, `analysis/actors/*.md`,
`analysis/threads/*.md`). This notice says what's stored, why, where it comes
from, and how to get your entry corrected or removed. It's written to meet
Mexico's *Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los
Particulares* (LFPDPPP), which is the law that applies to the person running
this project.

## Who is responsible

**Bernard Uriza Orozco**, Guadalajara, Jalisco, Mexico — bernarduriza@gmail.com.
He is the sole *responsable* (data controller) for everything in this repo.

## What's stored

For each person who has posted in one of the tracked public groups:

- display name as shown on Facebook, Facebook `user_id`, and the public profile URL;
- verbatim quotes of comments you posted **publicly** in those groups, with the
  thread they belong to;
- the maintainer's own working notes about your debate style: which side you
  argue, the tactics observed, and a one-line verdict used to decide whether and
  how to reply.

That third block is **opinion, not fact**. It's an analyst's read of public
arguments, kept so the maintainer doesn't repeat the same debate twice. It is
not a claim about who you are outside the thread.

Nothing here is collected from private messages, closed groups, friends-only
posts, or any surface that requires being on your friends list.

## Where it comes from

Only from **publicly accessible sources**: posts and comments you made in
Facebook groups that anyone can read. Under LFPDPPP article 10, section II,
personal data that appears in publicly accessible sources can be processed
without asking for consent first. This notice exists anyway, because you have
the right to know and to object.

If you also state a position on animal products in those posts (for or
against), that position is recorded exactly as you published it. It's treated as
your own public statement, never as an inference about your beliefs.

## What it's used for

- Preparing replies in the same public debates, aimed at the people reading
  along rather than at the person being answered.
- Remembering across threads who has already been answered, and how.
- Nothing else. No advertising, no profiling for any commercial purpose, no
  automated decisions with legal effect on you.

## Who it's shared with

No one. The data isn't sold, rented, or transferred. The repository is public
on GitHub, which means anyone can read it; that's disclosed here on purpose. If
that ever changes, this notice changes first.

## How long it's kept

For as long as the tracked groups stay active and the person keeps posting in
them. Dossiers of people who stop appearing aren't refreshed and can be pruned.

## Your rights (ARCO)

You can ask, at any time and for free, to:

- **Access** — see everything stored about you.
- **Rectify** — fix anything inaccurate.
- **Cancel** — have your dossier deleted.
- **Object** — have your data stopped from being used for a specific purpose.

Send the request to **bernarduriza@gmail.com** with the subject line
`ARCO request` and enough information to identify your entry (your Facebook
display name or profile URL is enough). You'll get an answer within **20
business days**, as the LFPDPPP requires. A deletion request is honored by
removing the dossier and the `actors.json` entry and committing that removal,
so the change is public too.

## Changes to this notice

Any change is committed to this file in this repository, so the history of the
notice is the git history of `PRIVACY.md`.
